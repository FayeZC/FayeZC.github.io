# Product

## Register

brand

## Users and purpose

Chi (Faye) Zhang's bilingual academic website, for researchers, collaborators, students and visitors reading publications, projects, research interests and personal writing. The existing source data and routes are authoritative. Preserve all personal facts, links, contact behavior, news expansion and both language trees.

## Confirmed design brief

The user supplied an explicit white-void spatial reference on 2026-11-27 and requested a working homepage prototype first. The space itself is the interface: sparse asymmetric text nodes, small floating computing artifacts, precise chrome lines, monochrome white and silver with rare electric blue. Use Helvetica Neue/system sans and system monospace. No room, horizon, dominant central object, glass panels, bento grid or conventional two-column marketing hero. Text must remain level and stable.

## Principles

Use real academic content without invented achievements. Prefer lightweight 2.5D CSS/SVG over WebGL. Keep decorative motion independent of content and input. Provide mobile reading order, keyboard access, visible focus and reduced-motion support. Do not deploy this prototype until requested.
