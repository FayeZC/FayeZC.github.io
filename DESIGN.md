# White void homepage

White #FFFFFF, soft white #F5F5F5, light grey #E8EAEC, silver #BFC3C8, chrome #969CA3, carbon #161616, electric blue #246BFF. User-specified white and monochrome take precedence over generic palette conventions.

A distributed first viewport contains identity, section index, a biography fragment, research directions, and small silver artifacts. Subsequent content remains directly on white with offset text columns and minimal dividers. No cards or glass. Body: Helvetica Neue / system sans; annotations: system monospace. Body text >=16px, readable line length <=70ch. Only decorative wrappers use depth transforms. Reduced motion freezes parallax, drift and data packets. Coarse-pointer devices use a static composition. CSS scoped through a dedicated homepage layout; existing article/publication/CV pages retain their original layout during this prototype.
